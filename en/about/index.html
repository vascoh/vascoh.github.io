About me

From operations to data — and from data to digital solutions

Senior Business Intelligence Consultant working in BI since 2019, specialised in Power BI and SQL Server. I build data models, dashboards and automated reporting for healthcare, banking, public administration and e-commerce — turning complex data into decisions people trust.

I studied Distribution and Logistics and spent years close to operations, sales and reporting — including running my own e-commerce business on marketplaces such as Amazon, eBay, Fnac and Worten, where KPIs, inventory and margins were part of every day. That's where I learned that data only matters when it answers a real business question.

In 2019 I moved into Business Intelligence and completed a postgraduate degree in Information Management and BI at NOVA IMS. Since then I've worked as a BI consultant at IDW (public administration), genesis.studio (banking) and, since 2022, Noesis — as Senior BI Consultant — with Power BI, SQL Server, DAX, Power Query, SSIS and SSRS, often on large data volumes.

I have a particular interest in performance tuning: finding the bottleneck in an execution plan, rewriting a slow process, making a heavy report feel instant. It's where analytical thinking and technical depth meet.

More recently, I've adopted Large Language Models as practical tools for productivity and development. They let me turn ideas into web applications and digital solutions faster — while I keep ownership of the analysis, architecture and validation.
